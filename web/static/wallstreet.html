📈 Wall Street Poker

Auction your way to a poker hand. 2–6 players · 🪙 buy-in

Sign in with Discord to play for casino coins.

How it plays. Everyone gets 2 hidden cards. Cards come out 3 at a time and are auctioned left to right: everyone who still needs cards submits a sealed bid, bids reveal together, the high bid takes the card face-up and pays the pot. Ties go to the seat closest left of the button.
No card is skipped: if nobody bids, the first seat left of the button eats it at the forced price.
Click anyone's face-up card to offer to buy it (paid to them, not the pot). At 5 cards you stop bidding unless you sell one. Fold any time to keep your stack.
Once everyone holds 5, best poker hand takes the pot. You cash out your stack + winnings.
