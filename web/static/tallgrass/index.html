    What's real

    • From the games: base stats, types, the damage formula, type chart, crits, accuracy, stat stages, PP, Struggle, status, weather, recoil, drain, multi-hit, one-hit KOs, Transform, split XP, level-up moves, evolution levels.
    • Wild armies: Red, Blue and Yellow encounter tables. Default moves follow the wild rule: the last four learned by level, from Crystal's learnsets (Emerald's for Gen 3 species).
    • Modern values: stats, moves, types, and abilities use current-generation numbers, so Clefairy is a Fairy type.
    • To scale: sprites and footprints use real Pokédex heights, so 44 Rattata can bite an Onix at once and only 8 can reach another Rattata. The smallest bodies are drawn at 0.42 m so you can still see them.
    • Duel prices: a formula from stats, level and moves, then corrected per species by simulating thousands of wars until no pure army dominates at equal money.
    • Invented for the map: tiles, walking speed, and move ranges. Everyone acts each turn in priority and Speed order.
    • Team tactics: Gens 1 to 3 have almost no way to help an ally, so Helping Hand, Follow Me, Rage Powder, Howl, Tailwind, Heal Pulse, Life Dew and Coaching come from each species' modern learnset. "Nearby" means within 3 metres.
    • Battle sets: "Battle set" picks a setup move for its main attack (Swords Dance, Dragon Dance, Calm Mind, Bulk Up), attacks of different types, and a heal, sleep, its own weather or a status move.
    • Not simulated yet: Protect, Substitute, Encore, Counter, Future Sight, Fake Out, Focus Punch, Belly Drum, Hail, entry hazards, held items, and about half of all abilities. The inspect card says when an ability has no effect.
