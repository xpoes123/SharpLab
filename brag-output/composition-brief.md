# Hyperframes Composition Brief: SharpLab

Objective: short brag video. Composition in composition/, render to brag.mp4. Source: web/static pages recorded on a local throwaway copy of a 2026-11-03 DB backup. Plan below is the contract.


## What is this app?
A "personal quantitative sports betting research lab" that grew into a Discord casino, a card economy, Pokémon basketball and a live election TV channel.

## The angle
The README is the joke. Line 5 says "Not a gambling product." The video enters that into evidence: each real page is an exhibit, relabelled in deadpan research language. Then the audit: 2.9M odds snapshots, 7,923 casino rounds, 81 bets logged.

## Hook
